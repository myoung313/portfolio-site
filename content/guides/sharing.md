title: Safer file sharing in OneDrive and SharePoint
summary: Sharing links, guest access, and finding what's shared.
---
## Pick the right kind of link
OneDrive and SharePoint offer three kinds of sharing link: Anyone (no sign-in needed, anyone with the link can open it), People in your organization, and Specific people. Use Specific people for anything sensitive. Anyone links are convenient but can be forwarded to anyone.

## Set safer defaults
In the SharePoint admin center under Policies, Sharing, you can change the default link type to Specific people, require Anyone links to expire after a set number of days, or turn Anyone links off completely. These settings apply to OneDrive too.

## Guests
Guests are outside people you invite to files, Teams or sites. Review guests every few months in the Microsoft 365 admin center under Users, Guest users, and remove ones you no longer work with.

## Find what's shared
Users can see what they've shared from OneDrive by opening Shared, then Shared by you. Remind your team to stop sharing files once a project ends.
