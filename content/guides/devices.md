---
title: Keep laptops and phones patched and protected
summary: Automatic updates, disk encryption, and lost-device plans.
---
## Turn on automatic updates
Most attacks use security holes that already have a fix. Turn on automatic updates for Windows, macOS, phones, browsers and Microsoft 365 apps, and restart regularly so updates actually install. Replace devices that no longer get security updates, such as PCs that can't run a supported version of Windows.

## Encrypt disks
Turn on BitLocker on Windows or FileVault on Mac so a lost or stolen laptop doesn't leak data. With Intune, the recovery keys can be stored in Entra ID automatically.

## Antivirus and firewall
Microsoft Defender Antivirus is built into Windows and is a good baseline. Microsoft 365 Business Premium adds Defender for Business, which can detect and respond to attacks across all your devices from one console.

## Manage devices centrally
Microsoft Intune, included in Business Premium, lets you enforce updates, encryption and screen locks, and wipe company data from a lost phone. If you can't afford it yet, keep a simple list of every device, who uses it, and whether updates and encryption are on.
