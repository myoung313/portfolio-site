---
title: Is free Gmail or Outlook.com okay for a business?
summary: Why a business domain and a business email plan are worth it.
---
## The problems with free personal email
A free personal address like yourbusiness@gmail.com looks less trustworthy to customers, is easy for scammers to imitate, and belongs to one person rather than the company. You also can't set SPF, DKIM and DMARC for a domain you don't own, and you have fewer admin controls, no central account recovery, and no clean way to hand over access when someone leaves.

## What a business plan adds
A plan like Microsoft 365 Business Basic, Standard or Premium, or Google Workspace, gives you email at your own domain, admin control over every account, MFA enforcement, retention and recovery options, and email authentication. It also keeps business data in business accounts.

## Making the move
Buy a domain if you don't have one, choose a business email plan, set up MFA from day one, and add SPF, DKIM and DMARC records. Keep the old free address forwarding to the new one for a few months and update your website, invoices and signatures.
