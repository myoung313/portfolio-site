How the check works

Your password is turned into a SHA-1 code in your browser. Only the first 5 characters are sent to Have I Been Pwned, which sends back hundreds of leaked codes that start the same way. The match happens on your device.

Why it matters

Leaked passwords are the first thing attackers try. A password that looked strong a few years ago may already be on a list.

What we store

Nothing. This page doesn't save, log or send your password, and the result isn't recorded anywhere.
