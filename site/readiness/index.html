Questions

Good to know

Is this insurance advice, or a guarantee I'll get covered?

No. It's a security readiness review that shows how your current setup lines up with what insurers commonly ask. Every insurer decides coverage and pricing on its own. I'm not an insurance agent and don't sell insurance.

Do I need to be technical to answer?

No. Each question is in plain English with a short explanation. If you don't know, pick "I'm not sure". Those go on a find-out list, and on the review call I can help you check.

How do I pay?

After I send your quote, you pay through a secure Stripe payment link. I never see or store your card details.

What do you do with my answers?

They're stored privately in Microsoft Azure, used only to make your report and to help you, and deleted on request. See the Privacy Policy.

Can you fix the gaps too?

Yes. Most fixes are Microsoft 365, Entra ID, Intune and Defender work, which is exactly what I do. Your report shows which items I can take off your plate.
