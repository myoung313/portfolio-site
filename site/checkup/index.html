SPF

A public list of the servers allowed to send email for your domain. Mail from anywhere else looks suspicious.

DKIM

A digital signature on every email you send, so receivers know it really came from you and wasn't changed.

DMARC

Your instructions to the world: if an email fails SPF and DKIM, send it to spam or block it. This is what actually stops spoofing.
