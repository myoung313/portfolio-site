Privacy Policy

Effective November 27, 2026

The short version

  • I only collect what you choose to send me, plus a small amount of technical data needed to run and protect the site.
  • I don't sell or rent your information, and I don't use advertising or tracking cookies.
  • Your data is stored in Microsoft Azure and is used only to reply to you, run the site's features and keep out spam.
  • You can ask to see, correct or delete your information at any time by emailing tyoung@cloudshiftlabs.net.

This policy explains how CloudShift Labs ("CloudShift Labs", "I", "me") handles information when you visit cloudshiftlabs.net (the "site"), including the contact form, the free email checkup, the Phish or Legit? game, the "Rate this site" widget, the cyber insurance readiness assessment and the client portal. Work done for clients under a signed agreement is covered by that agreement as well.

2. How I use it

  • To reply to your questions and requests, and to provide services you ask for.
  • To run the site's features and the client portal.
  • To keep the site secure, stop spam and fix problems.
  • To understand, in general terms, which features are useful so I can improve them.
  • To meet legal obligations, or to protect my rights or the safety of others when the law allows.

I won't add you to a mailing list unless you ask me to.

3. Who it's shared with

I don't sell, rent or trade your personal information. It is shared only with:

  • Microsoft, which hosts the site and its data (Azure Static Web Apps, Azure Functions and Azure Table Storage) and provides sign-in (Microsoft Entra ID). New comments are also checked by Microsoft's Azure AI Content Safety and Azure AI Language services to spot spam, abuse and tone. Microsoft processes data under its own privacy statement.
  • Stripe, which processes payments for paid services under its own privacy policy.
  • Authorities or others when the law requires it, or when needed to protect rights, property or safety.
  • A successor, if CloudShift Labs is ever merged or sold, under terms at least as protective as these.

4. Cookies and browser storage

The site doesn't use advertising, analytics or tracking cookies. It uses only what it needs to work:

  • Sign-in cookie (StaticWebAppsAuthCookie): set only when you sign in to the client portal, so the site knows you're signed in. Signing out removes it.
  • Browser storage on your device: whether you've already rated the site, whether this visit has already been counted (cleared when you close the tab), any of your own comments that are still waiting for review (so you can see them), your best game score, and a setting that plays the light-switch animation. This stays in your browser and isn't sent to me.

You can clear cookies and site data in your browser settings at any time. The portal won't work without the sign-in cookie.

5. How long I keep it

I keep information only as long as it's needed for the reasons above. Contact messages are kept for as long as they're useful for our conversation or any work that follows, and are deleted on request. Ratings, checkup records, the visit count and rate-limit records contain no names or emails. Readiness requests are kept while we are discussing or doing the work. Your assessment answers and report are kept while you are a client so you can come back to them, and are deleted within 90 days after you ask or after your portal access ends, unless a written agreement says otherwise. Payment records are kept as long as tax law requires. Public comments stay up until you ask me to remove them, or until I remove them myself; posts I reject are deleted from time to time. Portal access ends when I remove your role, and you can ask me to remove it at any time.

6. How it's protected

Data is sent over encrypted HTTPS connections and stored in Microsoft Azure, which encrypts data at rest. Access to the stored data is limited to me. IP addresses used for spam limits are stored only as one-way scrambled codes, not as the address itself. No system is perfectly secure, so I can't guarantee absolute security, but I'll tell affected people promptly if a breach involving their information occurs, as the law requires.

7. Your choices and rights

Wherever you live, you can ask me to:

  • tell you what information I hold about you, and give you a copy,
  • correct it,
  • delete it,
  • stop using it for a particular purpose.

Email tyoung@cloudshiftlabs.net with "Privacy request" in the subject. I'll reply within 30 days and may need to confirm your identity first. Depending on where you live (for example California or the European Union), you may have more rights under local law, and I'll honor them. I won't treat you differently for making a request.

8. Children

The site is for businesses and adults. It isn't directed to children under 13, and I don't knowingly collect their information. If you think a child has sent me information, email me and I'll delete it.

9. Where data is stored

CloudShift Labs is based in Indiana, in the United States. Your information is stored in Microsoft Azure data centers, which may be in the United States or other countries. By using the site, you understand your information may be processed there.

10. Other websites

The site links to other sites, such as GitHub and Microsoft Learn. Their privacy practices are their own, so please read their policies.
